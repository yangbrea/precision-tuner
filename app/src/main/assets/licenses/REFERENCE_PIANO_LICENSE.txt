Piano reference-tone samples
============================

Source: Splendid Grand Piano
  Public Domain samples released by AKAI in the early 2000s
  (Steinway piano, 4 velocity layers).
  Repackaged/converted by kinwie: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano
  ("Splendid Grand Piano.sfz", 256 MB version)

Used here: the MF (mezzo-forte) layer, 62 sampled keys MIDI 23..108,
decoded from FLAC to mono 44.1 kHz 16-bit WAV and trimmed to ~2 s for use
as the app's ear-tuning reference tone. Per the upstream README, modified
distributions should note that this sfz instrument is derived from the
Splendid Grand Piano repository.
